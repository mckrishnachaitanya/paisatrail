The balance you entered didn't match what PaisaTrail had calculated — usually because a few transactions weren't logged. We've added a one-off Balance Adjustment entry to make up the difference, so your balance now matches your bank.

Because we can't know exactly what caused the gap or when it happened, this entry won't show up in your insights or forecasts. You'll find it in the account's transaction list.
